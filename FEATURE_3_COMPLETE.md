## 🎮 **How to Use**

### **Connect Heart Rate Monitor:**
1. Turn on your Bluetooth heart rate monitor
2. Open GPS Track Run screen
3. Tap "Connect" in Heart Rate Monitor section
4. Wait for automatic device discovery
5. Monitor shows real-time BPM and zone

### **During Runs:**
- **Real-time HR display** with current BPM
- **Color-coded zones**: Resting (gray), Fat-burn (green), Aerobic (yellow), Anaerobic (orange), Max (red)
- **HR data automatically recorded** with GPS points
- **Stats display** shows current heart rate

### **After Runs:**
- **HR statistics** in run summary (avg, max, min HR)
- **Zone analysis** showing time spent in each zone
- **HR data saved** with complete run history
- **HR indicators** in recent runs list

## 💡 **Heart Rate Zones Explained**

Based on age-calculated max HR (220 - age):
- **🔘 Resting** (< 60%): Recovery and warm-up
- **🟢 Fat-burn** (60-70%): Optimal fat burning zone
- **🟡 Aerobic** (70-80%): Cardiovascular fitness
- **🟠 Anaerobic** (80-90%): High-intensity training
- **🔴 Max Effort** (90%+): Peak performance zone
